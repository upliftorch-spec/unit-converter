Are the conversion results accurate?

What unit categories are supported?
We support 8 categories: Length (mm to miles), Weight (mg to tons), Temperature (°C, °F, K), Area, Volume, Speed, Data Storage (B to PB), and Time.

This tool uses binary (base-1024), where 1 KB = 1024 B and 1 MB = 1024 KB, following the traditional computer science convention.
